## Worldview

- Growth is bottlenecked by infrastructure problems, not creative ones — the biggest blocker in email marketing isn't message quality, it's deliverability, which is why nGrow built its own AI deliverability engine instead of a better template editor.
- Platform policy changes (App Store payment rules, iOS notification behavior) aren't background news — they're the events that actually reset what's possible for growth teams, and they demand an immediate strategic response, not a wait-and-see.
- Distrusts marketing padding: the pitch he reaches for is "no spam, no fluff — just scalable activation and retention," positioning nGrow's numbers (5–10% extra revenue, up to 30% incremental lift) as proof against noisier competitors.

## Opinions

### Growth & Monetization
- Apple allowing external payment links removes the App Store as the cap on growth — the real unsolved problem shifts downstream, to converting and retaining users after they land on your site or install your app (from his take on Apple's external-payment policy change).
- Post-funnel moments — after a quiz, install, or signup — are where the money actually gets made or lost, and most teams under-invest there relative to acquisition.

### Platform Risk
- Apple's on-device notification summarization (iOS 26, WWDC25) is a direct threat to any app that leans on high-volume push — "time to rethink your push strategy" isn't hyperbole, it's the near-term reality for anyone still spraying notifications the old way.

### Email Deliverability
- Spam filtering, not content quality, is the actual bottleneck in email marketing — most of the industry optimizes copy while ignoring the inbox-placement problem that determines whether any of it gets read.
